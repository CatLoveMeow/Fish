FAQ

  • WAIT, DOES IT NEED MY PASSWORD? ▼

    Nope. FISH only reads the grades that are already visible on your screen. Your login credentials are strictly between you and SLCM.

  • WHERE DOES ALL MY DATA GO? ▼

    Nowhere. Every calculation happens locally on your own machine. We have no servers, and your data never leaves your browser.

  • DO YOU TRACK MY USAGE? ▼

    There's no telemetry in the code to remove. Read it yourself on GitHub.
